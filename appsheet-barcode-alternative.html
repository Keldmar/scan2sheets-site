Which One Should You Choose?

Choose SheetScan if...

  • You just need to scan barcodes & QR codes into Google Sheets fast without becoming a no-code developer.
  • You want hands-free continuous +1 counting for shelf stocktakes and warehouse receiving.
  • Your spreadsheet has 10,000–100,000 rows and you need instant offline search.

Choose AppSheet if...

  • You need complex multi-table relational apps, approval workflows, PDF invoice generation, or custom dashboards.
  • You need both iOS and Web app interfaces with role-based security filters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to change my Google Sheet layout to use SheetScan?

No. SheetScan adapts to any existing spreadsheet headers. Just pick your Barcode column, Quantity column, and optional Auto-Timestamp column — or create a ready-to-use sheet in 1 tap.

Can I use SheetScan for free?

Yes. SheetScan includes 20 free scans every day (reset at midnight UTC) and a built-in offline Demo Mode so you can test the scanner speed before connecting your Google account.
