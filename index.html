Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Scan2Sheets free to use?

Yes! Scan2Sheets is completely free without intrusive paywalls or ads. If you love the app and want to support ongoing development, voluntary supporter plans are available inside the app.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Absolutely. Scan2Sheets is built with an offline-first architecture (Room Database). You can scan in warehouses, basements, or offline areas; all records will queue and sync seamlessly when you're back online.

How is my Google data protected?

The app communicates directly with Google's official Sheets & Drive APIs via scoped permissions. We do not run intermediate databases, and your data remains strictly between your device and your Google Account.

What barcode types are supported?

QR Code, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Codabar, ITF, and Data Matrix.
