Which One Should You Choose?

Choose SheetScan if...

  • Your inventory already lives in Google Sheets and you want zero middleman databases.
  • You want predictable indie pricing ($29.99/year or free 20 scans/day) instead of per-seat SaaS bills.
  • You scan in basements or warehouses with dead Wi-Fi and need 100,000-row local lookups.
  • You want hands-free Auto +1 shelf counting on standard Android phones.

Choose Orca Scan if...

  • Your team uses iPhones (iOS) alongside Android devices.
  • You need GS1/UDI healthcare compliance or native webhooks to Power BI, SAP, or Salesforce.
  • You prefer managing users and permissions inside a dedicated cloud web console.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I migrate my existing inventory sheet from Orca Scan to SheetScan?

Yes. Export your Orca Scan sheet to Google Sheets (or open your existing Google Spreadsheet), connect it in SheetScan, and map your Barcode and Quantity columns in seconds.

Does SheetScan work without an internet connection?

Yes. SheetScan caches up to 100,000 rows locally on your phone using Android Room DB. Every offline scan is queued safely and merges back to Google Sheets automatically once Wi-Fi or mobile data returns.

Is there a free plan to test it before paying?

Yes — every user gets 20 free scans every single day (resetting at midnight UTC) plus an instant offline Demo Mode with zero sign-in required.
